This guide sets up your organisation **by hand**, one record at a time. It's useful for learning how the pieces fit together, or for small additions. If your organisation already has data, skip to the sections you need.

<Tip>
  Setting up a whole portfolio? [Guided onboarding](/getting-started/getting-set-up) is faster. Upload documents for Joule to read, or load everything from one file, then review and create it all at once.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Choosing a plan is optional during sign-up. If you clicked **Skip for now — set up billing later**, an organisation admin can subscribe at any time from **Settings** › **Plan & billing**. See [Plan & billing](/billing).
</Note>

Each create button opens a drawer on the right. Fill in the fields and click **Save**.

## Step 1: Create your accounts

Accounts are the top-level containers, typically one per customer or business entity.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Accounts">
    Select **Accounts** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an account">
    Click **Add account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Enter the account name (required). You can also set the entity type, tax ID (ABN or ACN) and a parent account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  For a business with subsidiaries, create the parent account first, then set it as the parent on each child account.
</Tip>

See [Creating Accounts](/guides/creating-accounts) for more.

## Step 2: Add sites

Sites are the physical locations where energy is used: offices, warehouses, stores, factories.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sites">
    Select **Accounts** in the sidebar, then the **Sites** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a site">
    Click **New Site** to open the **Create New Site** drawer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Enter the site name, choose its account (both required) and enter the address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 3: Set up connections

Connections are individual utility meter points: electricity `NMI`s, gas `MIRN`s or water meters.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connections">
    Select **Connections** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a connection">
    Click **New Connection** to open the **Create New Connection** drawer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Enter the connection identifier (for example the `NMI` or `MIRN`) and its utility.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link it to a site">
    The drawer doesn't ask for a site. On the connection's page, click **Add to Site**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Managing Connections](/guides/managing-connections) for more.

## Step 4: Create agreements

Agreements track energy contracts between your accounts and suppliers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Agreements">
    Select **Agreements** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an agreement">
    Click **Add agreement** to open the **Create New Agreement** drawer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Enter the agreement name and type (both required), then the account, supplier and contract dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add connections">
    On the agreement's page, add connections on the **Connections** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Managing Agreements](/guides/managing-agreements) for bulk-adding connections and managing tariffs.

## Step 5: Upload your first invoice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Uploads">
    Select **Invoices** in the sidebar, then the **Uploads** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your files">
    Click **Upload invoices** (the upload icon in the header), then drag invoice files onto the drop area or click it to choose them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let UtiliRead process them">
    UtiliRead extracts each invoice and matches it to the right account and connection. Follow progress in the upload queue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Open the invoice to check the extracted data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can also have retailers email invoices to your UMS inbox so they're processed automatically. See [Inbox](/inbox).
</Tip>

## What to do next

### Set up tariff rates

Configure tariffs for your agreements so UMS can check invoice charges against expected rates. See [Managing Tariffs](/guides/managing-tariffs).

### Check invoice validation

Open **Invoices** › **Validations** to find billing errors and tariff mismatches. See [Working the validation queue](/guides/invoice-validation).

### Run an agreement expiry report

Open **Reports** and run the **Agreement Connections Expiry and Billing Report** to find contracts nearing renewal, so you can start renegotiating early.

### Configure brokerage (if applicable)

If you earn commission on energy agreements, set up brokerage. See [Brokerage revenue and invoicing](/guides/brokerage-revenue-and-invoicing).

### Bulk import from CSV

For large portfolios, import accounts, sites, connections, tariffs and more from CSV. Open the account menu (your name at the bottom of the sidebar), select **Import**, then choose **Portfolio data** to open the **Bulk upload** wizard. The options you see depend on your permissions. To create a whole hierarchy from one file, use the [all-in-one onboarding upload](/guides/bulk-importing-data#onboarding-upload-all-in-one). See [Bulk Importing Data](/guides/bulk-importing-data).

### Invite your team

Add colleagues and control what each of them can access. See [Setting Up a User](/guides/setting-up-a-user).

### Subscribe to a plan

If you skipped the plan step, subscribe from **Settings** › **Plan & billing** at any time. See [Plan & billing](/billing).
