## Sidebar navigation

The left sidebar is the primary way to move around UMS. It is organised into four sections that mirror your energy management workflow:

| Section         | What it contains                                                                    |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **General**     | Dashboard, Inbox, Reports, Suppliers — your daily starting points                   |
| **Accounts**    | Accounts, Sites, Agreements, Contacts — your customer portfolio                     |
| **Connections** | Connections, Tariffs, Scenarios, Retail Accounts, Brokerage — the energy data layer |
| **Invoices**    | Invoices, UtiliRead, Validation, Batches — your billing workflow                    |

### Expanding and collapsing

The sidebar can be **expanded** (showing icon and label) or **collapsed** (showing icons only). Click the arrow at the bottom of the sidebar to toggle between modes. The collapsed sidebar gives you more screen space for data grids and detail pages.

## Global search

Press **Cmd+K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows/Linux) to open the global search — or click the **Search everything...** bar at the top of the sidebar.

Global search finds results across all modules simultaneously — accounts, sites, connections, invoices, agreements, suppliers, and more. Click any result to navigate directly to that record.

## Common page patterns

UMS uses consistent patterns across all modules so you only need to learn them once.

### List pages

Every module has a list page with:

- **Summary cards** at the top showing counts and growth trends
- **Data grid** with sortable columns, row selection, and pagination
- **Toolbar** with filter, column visibility, export, refresh, density, and search controls
- **Create button** (+) in the top-right corner

### Row actions

Each row in the data grid has action buttons:

| Icon       | Action          | Description                                                |
| ---------- | --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Arrow      | **See Details** | Navigate to the record's detail page                       |
| Pencil     | **Quick Edit**  | Open a side drawer to edit fields without leaving the list |
| Three dots | **More**        | Additional context-specific actions                        |

### Detail pages

Detail pages use a **tabbed interface** with:

- **Header** showing the record name, status, and action buttons
- **Tabs** for different aspects of the record (e.g. Overview, Sites, Agreements, Invoices)
- **Overview tab** with summary statistics and key information

### Drawers

When you click **Quick Edit** or **Create**, a **drawer panel** slides in from the right side of the screen. Drawers let you view and edit records without navigating away from your current page. Click **Save** to apply changes or **Cancel** / press **Escape** to close.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut               | Action                        |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Cmd+K** / **Ctrl+K** | Open global search            |
| **Escape**             | Close drawer or modal         |
| **Enter**              | Submit form or confirm action |

## Notifications

The notification icon in the bottom-left of the sidebar shows your unread notification count. Click it to open the notification drawer where you can see alerts for:

- Completed reports
- Invoice import results
- Agreement expiry warnings
- Brokerage calculation completions

Click any notification to navigate directly to the relevant page.
