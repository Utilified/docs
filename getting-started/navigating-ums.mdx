## Sidebar

The left sidebar is the main way to move around UMS. Your most-used pages sit at the top, followed by five sections:

| Section | What it contains |
| --- | --- |
| *(top)* | **Home**, **Inbox** and **Help** |
| **Portfolio** | **Accounts** (with Sites, Retail accounts, Contacts and Suppliers), **Connections** (with Meters, Tariffs, Outages and Data feeds) and, when enabled, **Solar watch** |
| **Operations** | **Invoices** and **Payments** |
| **Commercial** | **Agreements**, **Scenarios**, **Proposals** and **Market** |
| **Billing** | **Brokerage** |
| **Insights** | **Reports** and **ESG** |

You only see the modules your role and your organisation's plan give you access to.

Below the sections, **Pinned** holds the pages you've marked as favourites, and **Recent** lists the records you viewed most recently.

### Hubs and tabs

Each sidebar item opens a hub. A hub groups related pages as tabs along the top of the page. For example, **Connections** has tabs for **Connections**, **Needs setup**, **Meters**, **Tariffs**, **Outages** and **Data feeds**. Many list tabs also offer views such as **All** or **Active** to filter the list quickly.

### Expanding and collapsing

The sidebar can be expanded (icons and labels) or collapsed (icons only). Collapse it when you need more room for a data grid.

## Account menu

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open the account menu:

- **My Account** for your profile, security and notification preferences
- **Settings** for your organisation's settings
- **Import**, to bring in invoices, portfolio data or brokerage revenue and see past imports (see [Imports](/imports))
- **Reference library**
- **Contact Utilified** (see [Contact Utilified](/help))

## Search

Click **Search…** at the top of the sidebar, or press **⌘K** (Mac) / **Ctrl+K** (Windows and Linux), to open search. It finds pages and records across accounts, sites, connections, invoices, agreements, suppliers and more. Each group of results has a **See all** link that opens the full list, already filtered.

To ask a question instead, choose **Ask Joule** in the search results, or press **⌘↵** / **Ctrl+↵**. This opens a new Joule conversation with your question.

## Notifications

The bell beside the search box shows your notifications and a count of unread ones. Click a notification to go to the record it's about.

## Joule

Joule is the AI assistant built into UMS. Open it with the Joule button beside your name at the bottom of the sidebar, or press **⌘J** / **Ctrl+J**. It opens in a side panel and can answer questions about your data. If you have permission to edit, it can also propose changes, which run only after you approve them.

## Page patterns

Every module uses the same patterns, so you only need to learn them once.

### List pages

- A **data grid** with sortable columns, filters, row selection and export
- **Views** above the grid for common filters
- A labelled create button in the page header, such as **New Connection** or **Add account**
- A **See Details** action on each row that opens the record

### Detail pages

- A header with the record's name, status and actions
- Tabs for each part of the record. Some tabs have segments, which are smaller switches inside the tab. For example, a Connection's **Metering** tab has segments for its meters, reads and usage analytics.
- An **Overview** tab with the key facts and summary figures

### Drawers

Creating or quickly editing a record often opens a drawer from the right. Click **Save** to apply your changes. Click **Cancel** or press **Escape** to close it without saving.

### Getting help on a page

Pages with a help icon in the header open the matching page of these docs. **Help** in the sidebar links to these docs and, where your organisation has it turned on, lists the requests you've sent to Utilified. Choosing **Contact Utilified** in the account menu opens [Contact Utilified](/help) beside the page you're on, where your organisation has it turned on.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **⌘K** / **Ctrl+K** | Open search |
| **⌘↵** / **Ctrl+↵** (in search) | Ask Joule |
| **⌘J** / **Ctrl+J** | Open or close Joule |
| **Escape** | Close a drawer or dialog |
