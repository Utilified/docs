After you verify your email, UMS asks you to choose a plan on the **Choose your plan** page. Your plan sets your usage limits and which features you can use.

## The plan page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept the terms">
    Tick **I agree to the Terms of Service & Master Services Agreement**. **Subscribe** and **Skip for now** stay disabled until you do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare the plans">
    Each card shows the plan's price, a description, the features it includes (ticked) and its usage limits. The **Recommended** chip marks the suggested plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose one">
    - **Subscribe** takes you to secure checkout. When payment is confirmed, you continue to the next step.
    - **Request access** appears on plans you can't buy online. It opens a short form (**Your name**, **Email**, **Message (optional)**). Click **Send request** and the Utilified team gets in touch.
    - **Skip for now — set up billing later** finishes this step without a subscription.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If Utilified has already assigned your organisation a plan, the page says so and shows **Continue to setup** instead. No payment is needed.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Connections | Invoices per month | Active users |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $400 / month | 500 | 200 | 5 |
| Business | $1,500 / month | 2,500 | 1,000 | 25 |
| Enterprise | Shown on the plan page | 10,000 | 5,000 | 100 |

Starter doesn't include API access, the white-label portal or single sign-on. Business and Enterprise include every feature. A plan with custom pricing shows a pricing summary and **Request access** instead of a monthly price and **Subscribe**.

<Note>
  Plans are managed centrally, so names, prices and limits can change. The plan page always shows what's current for your organisation.
</Note>

## Usage limits

Your plan caps the number of connections, invoices per month and active users. An organisation admin can see live usage against each limit in **Settings** › **Plan & billing**.

## Changing your plan later

You aren't locked in. An organisation admin can open **Settings** › **Plan & billing** at any time:

- **Change plan** shows the plan options so you can switch.
- **Manage billing** opens your payment details and invoices.

If you skipped this step, subscribe from the same place whenever you're ready. See [Plan & billing](/billing).

<Tip>
  Not sure which plan fits? Start on the lowest plan that covers your current usage. You can upgrade when you need a gated feature or more headroom.
</Tip>
