**Plan & billing** is where org admins choose a plan, change plans and manage payment details. Open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar, choose **Settings**, then **Plan & billing** in the **Organisation** group.

## Who can manage billing

Only org admins see **Plan & billing**. Other users don't see the section in the Settings rail. If billing lapses, they're asked to contact an org admin.

## The Plan & billing section

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Current plan** | Your plan's name and status (**Active**, **Trial** or **Cancelled**), the monthly price and the next renewal date. During a commitment term it also shows *On a 12-month commitment until* a date. |
| **Manage billing** | Opens the Stripe billing portal in a new tab, where you update your payment method and see invoices. It appears once your organisation has a Stripe customer record. |
| **Usage** | Meters for **Connections**, **Users** and **Invoices this month** against your plan's limits, or **Unlimited**. |
| **Change plan** | Shows the plan picker, **Choose or change your plan**. Click **Hide plan options** to close it. |

### Billed directly by Utilified

If Utilified invoices your organisation directly, the section shows *"Your subscription is billed directly by Utilified. Contact us to change your plan."* **Manage billing**, **Change plan** and the plan picker are hidden.

## Upgrading from the free trial

On the [free trial](/getting-started/free-trial), **Current plan** shows the **Trial** status. When you reach a trial limit or try a feature the trial doesn't include, UMS opens an upgrade prompt. **Upgrade** brings you here, and **Not now** closes it. Only org admins can upgrade. Other users are asked to contact one.

To upgrade, choose a plan below and click **Subscribe**. Stripe Checkout takes your payment details, and when payment succeeds your organisation moves to the new plan with full access.

### When the trial ends

After 30 days, a trial organisation becomes read-only. Everyone can still sign in and see the data, but nothing can be added or changed. The banner at the top of the app reads *"Your free trial has ended. Your data is safe and read-only."*, and actions that would change data open the upgrade prompt.

Your data is kept for 60 days after the trial ends. If you haven't upgraded by then, it's deleted.

## Subscribe online

You can buy UMS without a trial or an existing account. Start from **Choose a plan** on utilified.com, or go to `ums.utilified.com/subscribe`. You can buy Starter or Business here. For Enterprise, click **Contact sales**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    On **Subscribe to Utilified**, pick a plan and enter your organisation name, your name and your work email. Click **Continue**. Personal email addresses, such as Gmail, can't be used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your email">
    Open the link in the email we send you. It expires after 7 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your subscription">
    Check your plan and organisation name, choose a password and accept the Terms & Conditions. Click **Continue to payment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay with Stripe">
    Stripe Checkout takes your card and billing address, and your ABN if you add one. When payment succeeds, Stripe returns you to UMS, and your organisation is ready within a few seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    When the page shows **You're all set**, click **Sign in** and use your email and the password you chose. You're the organisation's admin.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A plan bought online starts the [12-month commitment](#12-month-commitment) when payment succeeds. Refunds aren't available during the term.
</Note>

If your email already has a UMS account, we email you a sign-in link instead. Sign in and choose a plan here in **Plan & billing**. If your organisation already uses UMS, ask an org admin to invite you.

## Choose or change a plan

Each plan card shows the plan's name, price, features and limits. Your current plan shows **Current plan**, and some plans are marked **Recommended**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan picker">
    In **Plan & billing**, click **Change plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    Click **Subscribe** on the plan you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the change">
    - New subscribers go to Stripe Checkout to enter payment details. When payment succeeds, Stripe returns you to **Plan & billing** and the new plan is active.
    - Existing subscribers change plan in place on their current subscription. There's no redirect: a message confirms the change and the page shows the new plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  A plan change is prorated, so you pay only the difference for the rest of the current billing period.
</Tip>

### Request-only plans

Some plans, typically Enterprise, have custom pricing and can't be bought through Checkout. Their card shows a pricing summary instead of a monthly price, and a **Request access** button instead of **Subscribe**.

**Request access** opens a short form with **Your name**, **Email** and **Message (optional)**, filled in from your profile. Click **Send request**, and the Utilified team will follow up to set up the plan.

## 12-month commitment

Paid plans run on a 12-month commitment. While it lasts:

- Upgrades to a higher-priced plan are available at any time. An upgrade starts a new 12-month term from that day.
- Downgrades are locked until the term ends. Cheaper plans show a disabled button with *Committed until* the end date.
- Request-only plans aren't affected. You can send a request at any time.

When the term ends, it renews automatically for another 12 months. You can't cancel from within UMS. To end your subscription, contact Utilified before the term ends, and it stops at the end of the current term.

## When billing lapses

| Situation | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Billed directly by Utilified | Full access, no banner. |
| Subscription active or in trial | Full access, no banner. |
| Free trial running | Full access within the trial limits, with the trial banner. |
| Free trial ended | Read-only access, with the trial-ended banner. |
| A payment failed and is being retried | Full access, plus a warning banner on every page with an **Update billing** button. |
| The subscription has lapsed | The **Subscription action required** screen replaces the app. |

### Payment warning banner

When a payment fails and is still being retried, a banner reads *"We couldn't process your latest payment. Update your billing details to avoid interruption."* **Update billing** opens **Plan & billing**. You can dismiss the banner in that browser tab, but it comes back in new tabs until the payment is resolved.

### Subscription action required

If retries run out and the subscription lapses, a **Subscription action required** screen replaces the app.

- Org admins see **Manage billing**, which opens the Stripe billing portal to pay the overdue invoice or update the payment method, and **Choose a plan**, which opens the plan picker.
- Other users are asked to contact their organisation's administrator.

Both see **Sign out**.

<Note>
  Org admins can still open Settings and onboarding while access is paused, so they can resolve the payment. Every other page shows the lock screen until access is restored.
</Note>

## Billing during onboarding

Choosing a plan is optional when you sign up. On the plan step of onboarding you can pick a plan and complete Stripe Checkout, request access to a request-only plan, or click **Skip for now — set up billing later**.

If you skip, you can subscribe at any time from **Plan & billing**. If your organisation's access is later paused for billing, the **Subscription action required** screen asks an org admin to choose a plan.
