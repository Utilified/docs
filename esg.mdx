Select **ESG** in the **Insights** group of the sidebar (`/esg`).

<Note>
  ESG is a gated module. **ESG** only appears in the sidebar when the module is enabled for your organisation and your role includes the emissions permission. If you don't see it, contact your administrator.
</Note>

ESG tracks greenhouse gas emissions across Scope 1, Scope 2 (location and market) and Scope 3. You record activity data against reporting periods, calculate emissions with emission factors, review data quality, and prepare disclosures.

## Tabs

| Tab                   | Route                    | What it holds                                                    | Create button              |
| --------------------- | ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| **Overview**          | `/esg`                   | KPI cards and emissions charts                                    | —                          |
| **Reporting periods** | `/esg/reporting-periods` | The periods you report emissions against                         | **New Reporting Period**   |
| **Activity data**     | `/esg/activity`          | Activity records (fuel, electricity and so on) by scope and category | **New Activity Record** |
| **Targets**           | `/esg/targets`           | Emission-reduction targets with base and target values           | **New Target**             |
| **Disclosures**       | `/esg/disclosures`       | Disclosure content by section and reporting period               | **New Disclosure**         |
| **Scope 3**           | `/esg/scope3`            | Scope 3 materiality screening for an **Industry Sector**         | —                          |

Every tab also has a **Reference data** button in the header. See [Reference data](#reference-data).

## Overview

The **Overview** tab opens with KPI cards:

- **Total Emissions**: combined emissions for the latest active period, in tCO2-e
- **Active Periods**: reporting periods currently open
- **Sites**: sites available for emissions reporting
- **Open Review Items**: data-quality issues awaiting attention

Once you have data, the **Emissions by scope (latest period)**, **Emissions trend by scope** and **Emission reduction targets** charts appear below the cards.

## Reporting periods

Open a reporting period from the **Reporting periods** tab. The header shows its name, date range and status, and the page has **Overview**, **Calculations**, **Activity data**, **Review items** and **History** tabs.

### Actions

| Action                         | What it does                                                                     |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Calculate**                  | Queues a calculation that applies emission factors to the period's activity data |
| **Import Scope 2 from invoices** | Populates Scope 2 activity data from your invoices                             |
| **Run gap detection**          | Queues a check for missing data; findings appear on **Review items**             |
| **Generate ESG report**        | Opens **Reports** in a new tab                                                   |
| **Lock period** / **Unlock period** | Freezes the period's data once reporting is final, or reopens it            |

**Calculate** is the header's main button. The others are icon buttons; hover one to see its name. **Calculate** and **Import Scope 2 from invoices** are disabled while the period is locked.

### Statuses

A reporting period moves through **Open**, **Collecting data**, **Calculating**, **In review**, **Submitted** and **Locked**. **Locked** is final: the period's data is frozen until you unlock it.

### Working through a period

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the period">
    On the **Reporting periods** tab, click **New Reporting Period** and set its name, date range and applicable frameworks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add activity data">
    Add records on the **Activity data** tab, or use **Import Scope 2 from invoices** on the period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Calculate emissions">
    Click **Calculate**. Results appear on the **Calculations** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check for gaps">
    Run **Run gap detection**, then work through the **Review items** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lock the period">
    Use **Lock period** once reporting is final. The **History** tab records changes to the period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reference data

Click **Reference data** in the header of any ESG tab (`/esg/reference-data`). This page holds the shared definitions your calculations draw on. Use the **Reference data type** control in the grid toolbar to switch between types; the create button changes to match.

| Type                  | What it holds                                                        | Create button            |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| **Factor Sets**       | Named sets of emission factors, with source, reporting year and effective dates | **New Factor Set** |
| **Emission Factors**  | Individual factors by scope, category and region. You add them inside a factor set. | **New Factor Set** |
| **Equipment**         | Equipment, with type, fuel type and refrigerant type                 | **New Equipment**        |
| **Instruments**       | Environmental instruments (for example certificates) with quantities and vintage year | **New Instrument** |
| **Intensity Metrics** | Denominators used to normalise emissions                             | **New Intensity Metric** |
