Find it in the sidebar under **Commercial** › **Proposals**.

The Proposals module lists every assessment proposal in your organisation, newest first. A proposal starts from an assessment of a [site](/sites) and can draw on tariff reviews and solar and battery studies from [Scenarios](/scenarios).

The list shows each proposal's **Title**, **Site**, **Status**, **Issued** date and **Prepared by**.

<Note>
  Proposals only appears when the Proposals module is enabled for you. Creating a proposal also needs create access to both Modelling and Proposals. Without it, the **New proposal** button is hidden. Ask an organisation admin.
</Note>

## Creating a proposal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start an assessment">
    Click **New proposal**. UMS opens the **New assessment** wizard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the site">
    On the **Site** step, select the site to assess. UMS skips this step when the site is already chosen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the assessment details">
    On the **Assessment** step, choose the **Template** and the **Assumptions**.

    Enter a **Proposal title**, or leave it blank to use the suggested one. You can change it in the editor.

    For the **Baseline period**, keep the default period or pick **Choose dates** and set **From** and **To**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    **What happens when you create it** summarises what the assessment will do. Click **Create assessment**. UMS opens the draft proposal for you to review before anything is issued.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Starting from a study

You can also start a proposal from a completed tariff review or solar and battery study. On its **Overview**, click **Create proposal**. The wizard opens with that study attached and lists it under **What happens when you create it**.

When a tariff review is attached, the proposal reuses its saved assumptions and results. **Assumptions** is hidden and the baseline period is fixed to the review's period.

## Editing a proposal

Click a row in the list to open the proposal editor. The editor has three panes:

- **Sections**: the proposal's sections and how many are in the document. Select one to work on it.
- **Preview**: the proposal as it will read. Switch between **Fit width** and **Actual size**.
- The section inspector: the selected section's settings. Turn **Include in this proposal** on or off, review its warnings, and write its **Introduction**. The cover section also holds the **Proposal title**, **Template**, **Preparer name** and **ABN**.

On narrower screens the panes become tabs: **Sections**, **Preview** and **Edit section**.

Changes save automatically. The readiness bar shows **Saving** or **Saved**. If a save fails, it shows the error. Click **Retry**.

Joule can draft narrative text from the proposal's figures. Select a section and click **Draft with Joule**. Once Joule has drafted a section, the button reads **Regenerate with Joule**. To draft every section at once, click **Draft all with Joule** in the readiness bar. Wait for the analysis to finish before you ask it to draft. If you have already written text in a section, UMS asks before replacing it.

The **Tariff options**, **Power factor and demand** and **Solar and battery** sections list their costed options under **Options shown**. Tick the options the section presents. At least one stays shown.

## Issuing a proposal

The readiness bar shows **Ready to issue** once the proposal is complete. Otherwise it shows each warning as a chip. On narrower screens a single chip counts the warnings, for example **2 to resolve**, and opens the list.

Click a warning to open its section. Some warnings can be acknowledged instead of fixed. Clicking one of those also opens the acknowledge dialog, or click **Acknowledge** on it in the section inspector. Enter a **Note** explaining why it's acceptable, then click **Acknowledge**. The note is printed in the proposal's assumptions section. Acknowledging doesn't change the figures. Other warnings must be fixed.

Once every warning is resolved, click **Issue proposal**. The **Issue proposal?** dialog summarises the proposal. Click **Issue proposal** to confirm. Issuing freezes the document as previewed and creates its PDF. An issued proposal can't be edited or deleted.

An issued proposal opens for anyone signed in to your organisation. Once issued, the readiness bar offers:

- **Download PDF**: downloads the issued proposal.
- **Copy link**: copies a link that opens the proposal for anyone signed in to your organisation. Public client links aren't available yet.

If issuing takes longer than expected, nothing has been issued yet. Click **Issue again**.

## Statuses

| Status      | Meaning                                                    |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Draft**   | Being prepared. You can edit it                            |
| **Issuing** | Freezing the document and creating its PDF. Editing is paused |
| **Issued**  | Final. It can't be edited                                  |
