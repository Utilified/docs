The Proposals module lists every assessment proposal in your organisation, newest first. A proposal starts from an assessment of a [site](/sites) and can draw on solar and battery studies from [Scenarios](/scenarios).

<Note>
  Proposals only appears when the Proposals module is enabled for you. Creating a proposal also needs create access to both Modelling and Proposals. Without it, the **New proposal** button is hidden. Ask an organisation admin.
</Note>

## Creating a proposal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start an assessment">
    Click **New proposal**. UMS opens the **New assessment** wizard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the site">
    Select the **Site** to assess.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the assessment details">
    Choose the **Template**, the **Baseline period** and any **Assumptions**. You can attach up to five existing studies.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing a proposal

Click a row in the list to open the proposal editor. The editor has three panes:

- **Sections**: the proposal's sections. Select one to change it under **Edit section**.
- **Preview**: the proposal as it will read.
- **Section settings**: options for the selected section.

Changes save automatically. The header shows **Saving** or **Saved**.

Joule can draft narrative text from the proposal's figures. Wait for the analysis to finish before you ask it to draft. If you have already written text in a section, UMS asks before replacing it.

## Issuing a proposal

The readiness bar shows **Ready to issue** once the proposal is complete, or lists **Warnings to resolve**. Acknowledge or fix each warning, then issue the proposal.

An issued proposal opens for anyone signed in to your organisation. A proposal shows as **Draft** until you issue it, then as **Issued**.
