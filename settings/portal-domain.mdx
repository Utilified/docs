The **Portal & domain** section of [Settings](/settings), in the **Access & integrations** group, runs your white-label customer portal on a domain you control. The same domain carries inbound and outbound email. The section has five parts:

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [**Domain**](#add-a-custom-domain) | Attach a custom domain, delegate its nameservers and turn on email delivery. |
| [**Deployment**](#deploy-the-portal) | Stand up a dedicated portal instance on that domain. |
| **Theme** | Branding for the portal. See [Portal theme and single sign-on](/settings/white-label#portal-theme). |
| [**Client plans**](#client-plans) | Named packages of portal features you assign to clients. |
| [**Default portal access**](#default-portal-access) | What portal users see when their account has no settings of its own. |

## Who can use this

- Only org admins see **Portal & domain**.
- It is a Business and Enterprise feature. Organisations on the Starter plan don't see the section. Organisations with no plan attached are treated as entitled and do see it.
- To get it, upgrade from **Settings** › **Plan & billing**. See [Plan & billing](/billing).

If the plan doesn't include the portal, **Deployment** shows *"Portal deployment is available on the Business and Enterprise plans."* with an **Upgrade** button that opens **Plan & billing**.

## Add a custom domain

Use a subdomain you control, such as `portal.yourcompany.com.au`. One domain powers the portal URL, outbound email and DKIM signing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the domain">
    Under **Domain**, enter your domain in the **Domain** field and click **Add domain**. UMS creates a hosted DNS zone for it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delegate your nameservers">
    **Delegate your domain** lists four nameservers. At your domain registrar, set the nameservers for your domain to those four. If your registrar separates custom nameservers from DNS records, use the nameservers setting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for verification">
    Delegation usually propagates in 5–60 minutes. Once it does, Utilified manages DKIM, SPF, MX and your SSL certificate, and the status changes to **Verified**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Utilified uses nameserver delegation, not record-by-record setup. You won't be asked to publish individual CNAME, TXT or MX records for the portal domain.
</Note>

## Verify your domain

The status badge next to your domain shows where verification is:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Awaiting DNS** | UMS hasn't seen your nameserver delegation yet. |
| **Verified** | Delegation is in place. You can deploy the portal and turn on email. |
| **Verification failed** | The check failed. Most often the nameservers don't match. |

While the badge shows **Awaiting DNS**, UMS checks every 30 seconds without a refresh. It pauses while the browser tab is hidden. After about ten minutes it stops checking on its own and asks you to click **Check now** to keep going. **Check now** also runs a check straight away and reports the result.

## Deploy the portal

**Deployment** needs a verified domain with its hosted zone and SSL certificate in place. Until then it shows **Prerequisites required**, with a **Configure domain** button that takes you back to the domain setup.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the deployment">
    Click **Deploy portal**. Hover the button to see why it's disabled, if it is.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The **Deploy portal** dialog shows the address the portal will run at. Click **Deploy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for it to finish">
    Deployment usually takes 3–5 minutes. A progress stepper shows each stage. When it finishes, the portal's URL appears with a copy button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once the portal is deployed, click **Update** to redeploy it or **Delete portal** to remove it. Deleting can't be undone, but you can deploy again later. If a deployment fails, click **Retry deployment**. Use the refresh icon beside the status badge to check the latest status.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Not deployed** | No portal exists yet. |
| **Deploying…**, **Updating…**, **Deleting…** | A change is in progress. The buttons are disabled until it finishes. |
| **Deployed** | The portal is live at your domain. |
| **Deployment failed**, **Update failed**, **Delete failed**, **Deployment stuck** | Something went wrong. Click **Retry deployment**, or contact Utilified support if it keeps failing. |
| **Deployment rolled back**, **Update rolled back** | The change failed and was undone. Click **Retry deployment**. |

## Inbound and outbound email

**Email delivery** appears once you have added a domain. It has two switches:

- **Outbound email**: send notifications from `no-reply@yourdomain`.
- **Inbound email**: accept invoices and other mail at addresses like `invoices@yourdomain`.

Both need a verified domain. Until then, each switch is disabled and notes *"Requires DNS verification."*

Turning a switch on takes effect straight away. Turning one off asks you to confirm first: **Turn off outbound email?** or **Turn off inbound email?** Click **Turn off** to go ahead.

<Tip>
  Turn on outbound email first to start sending branded notifications. Turn on inbound email when you're ready to receive mail, such as supplier invoices, at your domain.
</Tip>

## Change or remove your domain

You can't rename a domain in place.

- **Change domain** tears down the current domain, including its portal and email verification, so you can add a new one. You then delegate the new domain's nameservers and wait for verification again.
- **Remove domain** (the bin icon next to the status badge) reverts your portal to the default Utilified domain and tears down email verification.

Both open a confirmation dialog (**Change domain?** or **Remove domain?**) that warns the action can't be undone. It lists what will stop, such as inbound or outbound email on the domain.

## Client plans

A client plan is a named package of portal features. Assign a plan to a client's account, or to the default portal access, and the plan decides which features that client's portal shows. Plans also carry a price and say how you charge for them. For the full workflow, see [Offering portal plans to your clients](/guides/packaging-portal-access).

The **Client plans** table lists each plan with its **Name**, **Price**, **Charge mode** and **Clients**, the number of client accounts using it.

### Create or edit a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan">
    Click **New plan**, or **Edit** on a plan's row. When you edit a plan that's in use, the dialog shows how many clients it applies to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    Enter a **Name** and, if you like, a **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the price">
    Enter a **Price** and choose a **Billing period** of **Monthly** or **Annual**. Turn off **GST applicable** if the plan is GST-free. You can leave the price empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how it's charged">
    Set **Charge mode** to one of the options below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set each feature">
    The feature table lists every portal feature. Set each one to **Included**, **Locked** or **Hidden**, then click **Create plan** or **Save plan**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Charge mode | How the client pays |
| --- | --- |
| **Brokerage** (the default) | Through an energy or metering brokerage contract you already hold for the client. You record the plan's share of the contract rate on the account. See [Brokerage recovery](/accounts#brokerage-recovery). |
| **Service fee** | Directly. UMS generates a monthly fee you raise in Xero. See [Service fees](/brokerage#service-fees). |
| **None** | You don't charge for the plan through UMS. |

### Included, locked and hidden

| Access | What the client sees in their portal |
| --- | --- |
| **Included** | The feature, working as normal. |
| **Locked** | The feature in the menu with a lock. Opening it shows a description of the feature and a **Request a quote** button. The request reaches you on the client's account. See [Quote requests](/accounts#quote-requests). |
| **Hidden** | Nothing. The feature doesn't appear. |

<Note>
  A locked feature never shows the client's data. The portal shows only the feature's name and description until you include it in the client's plan. The quote page doesn't show plan names or prices either.
</Note>

### Delete a plan

Click **Delete** on the plan's row, then **Delete plan** to confirm. You can't delete a plan while any client or the default portal access uses it. Move those clients to another plan first.

### Inactive plans

To make a plan inactive, edit it and turn off **Active**. Turn it back on to offer the plan again.

An inactive plan shows **(inactive)** after its name. It stays on every client already using it, and those clients keep its features. You can't assign it to another client, and it doesn't appear in the **Plan** picker or in CRM plan mappings.

<Note>
  Making a plan inactive doesn't change what existing clients see. To move a client off it, choose another plan on the client's account.
</Note>

## Default portal access

**Default portal access** sets what portal users see when their account has no portal access settings of its own. An account's own settings, in the **Portal Access** section of its **Settings** tab, override these defaults. See [Accounts](/accounts#settings-tab).

- **Plan**: choose a client plan for every account without settings of its own, or **Custom** to use the switches below. With a plan chosen, its features show read-only under **Plan features** instead of the switches.
- **Single sign-on**: **Let portal admins set up single sign-on** lets portal admins connect their own company's Microsoft sign-in for their account and its sub-accounts.
- **Portal Features**: turn portal areas on or off, such as **Validation outcomes**, **Invoices**, **Connections & meters**, **Agreements** and **Reports**. Everything is on unless you switch it off. Features marked **(opt-in)**, such as **Budgets (opt-in)** and **Dashboards (opt-in)**, stay off until you switch them on.
- **Portal Reports**: the reports portal users can run. Standard reports are on unless you switch them off, and opt-in reports are off unless you switch them on. Without a plan, this only applies while **Reports** is on.

Click **Save Portal Defaults** to apply your changes.

## Troubleshooting

### The section is missing

**Portal & domain** is for org admins on a plan that includes the portal. Upgrade from **Settings** › **Plan & billing**, or ask an org admin.

### The status stays on Awaiting DNS

Check at your registrar that the domain's nameservers exactly match the four shown in UMS. A trailing dot makes no difference. Delegation can take up to an hour.

### The status shows Verification failed

This is usually a nameserver mismatch, or a registrar that hasn't propagated the change. Check the nameservers and click **Check now**.

### Inbound email won't turn on

If turning on **Inbound email** shows "This domain is already enabled for another organisation.", another Utilified organisation already receives email at the domain. Turn it off there first, or choose a different subdomain.

### Deploy portal stays disabled with a verified domain

Hover the button for the reason. Usually a deployment is already in progress.
