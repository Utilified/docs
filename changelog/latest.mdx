Stay up to date with the latest features and improvements in UMS.

<Update label="v4.27.0" description="2026-11-14">
  ## Usage analytics, a Forwards tab and supply register, and more reliable exports

  This release covers everything since v4.17.0.

  ### Connections and sites

  - **Usage analytics for Connections** — Open a Connection's Metering tab and choose Usage analytics to see interval usage against your tariff's time-of-use periods, 12 months of daily usage split by period, an average weekday and weekend day, an hour-by-date heatmap, and your measured monthly peak against the demand charges that apply.
  - **Usage analytics for Sites** — The Site Usage tab rolls the same views up across a Site's Connections, with the Site's coincident peak demand beside the sum of its Connection peaks and each Connection's contribution at the peak.

  ### Market

  - **Forwards tab** — Futures contracts, strips, forward curves and settlement history now live in one place. WEM curves are labelled as Synergy's offered prices.
  - **Supply tab** — See generation leaving and arriving by year, fuel and technology, the committed and announced pipeline, and the next closure and next arrival in each region.
  - **Clearer market desk** — Price changes compare like with like and say so, spot changes show in $/MWh, news reads as one tidy list, insight signals state direction and size, and Analyse charts share one date axis.
  - **ARENA news** — News from the Australian Renewable Energy Agency joins the market news feed.

  ### Invoices and validation

  - **Supplier credits** — Credits are booked with net settlement, and a repeated credit that could post twice is held for review instead.
  - **Red Energy CSVs** — Usage and line quantities are recorded from Red Energy CSV invoices.
  - **Origin rebills** — An Origin cancel-and-rebill that arrives in a separate file is linked to the bill it replaces.
  - **UtiliRead balance check** — Other charges shown inside current charges no longer fail the balance check.
  - **Per-unit daily rates** — Validation prices daily rates with no binding over the days in the period.

  ### Payments

  - **Consolidated accounts** — Consolidated Retail Accounts are coded per meter in payment coding.

  ### Reports and exports

  - **Grid exports** — Grid exports now complete and email your download link.
  - **Export size limit** — Exports and scheduled reports covering more than 2,000 Connections are refused with a message asking you to filter by Account, Site or Connection.
  - **Export notifications** — You're notified only once the export file is ready.
  - **Account uploads and stats** — Account CSV uploads report unsupported rows accurately, and Account stats count Retail Account and Agreement links.

  ### Brokerage

  - **Preview draft invoice amendments** — Preview corrections to a manually raised draft brokerage invoice before anything changes.
  - **Contract matching on upload** — Uploads prefer open contracts over closed ones.
  - **Delete Rows** — Delete Rows no longer reports success on draft brokerage rows it didn't delete.
  - **Faster overview** — The brokerage overview loads faster and shows its layout while figures load.

  ### Settings

  - **SSO callback URLs** — The SSO setup wizard shows every callback URL your Microsoft Entra application needs, with copyable guidance.

  ### Other improvements

  - **Page scrolling** — The page no longer scrolls behind the app.
  - **Spend charts** — Tidier legends, labels and axis colours.
</Update>
