Stay up to date with the latest features and improvements in UMS.

<Update label="v4.48.1" description="2026-11-24">
  ## Change history, per-channel notifications and attachments on support requests

  This release covers everything since v4.42.0.

  ### Accounts, sites and connections

  - **Change history** — See who changed which fields on an Account, Site, Agreement or Connection, and when. Agreements show it on an Activity tab, and Accounts, Sites and Connections show it under Change history in their Settings tab.
  - **Readable history** — History shows each change as its old and new value, names the person who made it, names linked records instead of showing ids, and records bulk uploads as a "Bulk import" by the person who uploaded the file.

  ### Settings

  - **Notification settings per channel** — Settings, Notifications lists each notification type with Email and In-app checkboxes, so you can switch off one channel for a notification and keep the other.

  ### Help

  - **Attachments on support requests** — Attach a bill, a screenshot or another file when you contact Utilified, and we can send files back to you.

  ### Reports and ESG

  - **Billing-period custom reports** — Custom reports built on Connection billing periods count rebills and reversals the same way the invoice ledger does.
  - **Renewable energy certificates** — Each day's certificate kWh is shared across all of a Connection's Scope 2 activity, so adjustments and partial credits no longer overstate it. The ESG section appears only when it is enabled for your organisation.

  ### Other improvements

  - **Clickable tables** — Links in table cells are underlined, and rows you can open show a pointer.
  - **Faster Meter and Register lists** — The Meters and Registers lists load faster on large portfolios.
  - **SSO sign-in** — SSO sign-ins only redirect to your identity provider's own address.
</Update>
