Stay up to date with the latest features and improvements in UMS.

<Update label="v3.10.2" description="2026-10-23">
  ## Billing gaps, an agreement onboarding pipeline, Scenario Studio and brokerage runs

  ### Invoices and validation

  - **Billing gaps** — A new Billing gaps page under Invoices shows, period by period, which bills each Connection has received and which are partial, missing or not yet due. Accounts, Connections and Retail Accounts show the same completeness view.
  - **Request missing bills** — Select the gaps you want filled and send a bill request to the retailer from UMS.
  - **Preview a tariff fix before you save** — The Fix tariff drawer now prices your edits against the real invoice, so you can see the result before the change is saved.
  - **Dismissed exceptions stay dismissed** — An exception you dismiss is no longer raised again by the daily and weekly checks.
  - **Fewer stuck tariff reviews** — Older bills that arrive after newer ones now price against the right tariff, and tariff reviews are no longer raised on accounts with validation turned off or where no tariff priced anything.
  - **More accurate network validation** — Network charges are checked more precisely, including kVA demand, capacity ratchets (such as Ausgrid EA302, EA305 and EA310), per-meter charges, reactive export, jurisdictional scheme lines, TasNetworks specified demand, AusNet critical peak demand, UFE charges, TOU apportionment and discounts.
  - **Energex large-business tariffs** — Energex large-business network TOU tariffs are now available.

  ### Agreements

  - **Onboarding pipeline** — A new pipeline view tracks each agreement Connection from submission to in-service, with milestone dates, days in stage and overdue flags. Contestable metering eligibility is tracked per Connection.
  - **Retailer submission pack** — Email the signed retail agreement, plus any overlapping metering agreements, to the retailer from the agreement.

  ### Brokerage

  - **Brokerage runs with preview** — Preview a brokerage run as a draft before you commit it, see coverage and gaps, and rerun past periods when source data changes.
  - **Three rate types** — Contracts use per-unit, percentage or fixed-fee rates. Percentage commission can come from invoices or be calculated from tariffs.
  - **Supplier statements (RCTIs)** — Upload RCTIs from retailers and metering providers, check them against calculated revenue and accept them as what the supplier paid. Metering revenue now starts at the install date.
  - **Raise an invoice across suppliers** — Pick several suppliers, or specific child suppliers, when you raise an invoice from unbilled revenue.
  - **Brokerage fixes** — Supplier tabs count contracts correctly, revenue labels are clearer, bulk transitions ask for confirmation, Xero status sync reaches every open invoice, and LPG revenue rolls up to calendar months.

  ### Payments and ledger

  - **Outstanding from the latest bill** — For a Retail Account that isn't on a payment file, Outstanding shows the latest bill's amount due and is labelled so it isn't read as the ledger total.
  - **SAP journal coding holds** — An SAP journal payment group holds an invoice whose Site or Account is missing coding, instead of failing the whole file or posting a blank cost centre.
  - **Validation holds** — A payment group can hold back invoices whose validation failed or is still unconfirmed, and the coverage reason says why.

  ### Scenarios

  Scenarios require modelling access for your organisation.

  - **Scenario Studio** — One launcher replaces the five scenario cards. Pick a Site or Retail Account and what you want to explore, then run once. Results are redesigned and include existing solar and batteries.
  - **Portfolio screening** — The portfolio screen now screens batteries and power factor correction alongside solar.

  ### Settings

  - **Automations** — Set up scheduled automations that generate Retail Accounts or email retailer submission packs. Every automation starts off. This section appears only when automations are enabled for your organisation.

  ### Other improvements

  - **Global search** — Search (⌘K) now covers Accounts, Sites, Connections, Retail Accounts, Invoices, Agreements, Suppliers and Contacts, grouped by type, with a See all link and an Ask Joule option.
  - **No duplicate Connections** — A gas MIRN labelled as an NMI, in an upload or an invoice, no longer creates a second Connection, and read uploads match the right Connection.
  - **Meters** — A new Meter starts when a meter's interval length changes.
  - **Reports** — Report account filters include the whole account hierarchy.
  - **UtiliRead** — Supplier matching prefers the Retail Account's own supplier and only fuzzy-matches the same brand, and credit-note connection charges come from the bill's lines.
  - **Forms** — Field labels line up with their inputs, including multi-line labels.
  - **Security** — Sessions, SSO and login throttling are hardened.
</Update>
