Stay up to date with the latest features and improvements in UMS.

<Update label="v3.3.1" description="2026-10-17">
  ## Utilified platform 3.0: a new app layout, Site and Connection analytics, and SolisCloud integration preparation

  UMS now ships as part of the Utilified platform, with one version number for everything. That's why this release jumps from v1.69.0 to v3.3.1.

  ### Navigation and layout

  - **New app layout** — Global actions now live in the sidebar and every page shares one frame, so the desktop top bar is gone and more of the screen goes to your data.
  - **Navigation improvements** — Back, Cancel and Close have more consistent destinations in supported flows, including a parent-page fallback when you open a link directly.
  - **Regrouped Settings** — Settings sections are grouped under Account, Organisation, Access & integrations, Operations and Commercial, with icons and search, and every section uses the same layout.

  ### Sites and Connections

  - **Site analytics** — A Site's Overview shows its spend and usage at a glance, plus a monthly spend and usage chart.
  - **Connection analytics** — A Connection's Overview shows spend, usage and contract details, with a data-feed status panel and when the first bill is expected.
  - **Spend from every bill** — Spend charts now include bills that have no billing period, and the Home spend chart uses the same organisation-wide monthly trend.
  - **SolisCloud integration preparation** — Preparation for SolisCloud solar data feeds is in place. Customer availability is pending production activation and live validation.

  ### Invoices

  - **Origin LPG reports** — Import Origin LPG monthly transaction reports and usage reports. Rows link to Connections by location number, and any skipped rows are reported.
  - **Find identifiers however they're typed** — Search matches NMIs and other identifiers with or without spaces and punctuation, and invoice search now covers NMI, Site, parent account and aliases.
  - **Preview an inbox exclusion** — Before you exclude an inbox email, see which downstream records the exclusion would leave behind.
  - **Smarter missing-bill checks** — UMS reads each Retail Account's billing cadence from its recent bills and statements, and only flags a missing invoice once the next bill is overdue.
  - **No duplicate paid bills** — UMS blocks re-importing a bill that has already been paid.

  ### Payments and ledger

  - **Add an invoice to a payment file** — Add a missing invoice to a payment file before it is released. Once a file is released, its lines are locked.
  - **Clearer coverage reasons** — Coverage flags explain when an invoice falls outside the Retail Account's active window, and such invoices are held only when the mismatch is confirmed.
  - **Delete a ledger row written in error** — Remove an invoice-derived row from a Retail Account's Ledger tab.

  ### Other improvements

  - **Consistent formatting** — Money and brokerage rates are formatted the same way across UMS, and dashboard cards fill the full width.
  - **Accrual report** — Reversed bills no longer appear in the accrual report.
  - **Agreements** — Retail Accounts closed before an agreement's term are ignored.
  - **UtiliRead** — Credit balances are extracted as negative amounts, and non-invoice correspondence is skipped at import.
  - **Read coverage** — Current-month read coverage is measured against the days elapsed, and daily summaries count consumption only.
  - **Inbox previews** — Email previews no longer pick up UMS styles.
  - **Security** — Changing your password ends your other sessions, and SSO user mappings are read-only in Settings and used for sign-in only.
</Update>
