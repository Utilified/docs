  ## Existing equipment comparisons and in-credit invoice selection

  ### Energy assessments

  Energy assessments and existing-equipment comparisons require modelling and DER-modelling access for your organisation, plus the relevant user permissions. Proposals appear only when proposal access is also enabled for your organisation and user.

  - **Compare existing equipment** — Compare today's operation with using selected existing equipment, then assess the additional value of new solar or battery options separately.
  - **Existing generator operating costs** — Model generators already at the Site using their declared operating limits and fuel costs. Results show fuel use, run hours, starts and Scope 1 emissions where supported by the study inputs.
  - **Tariffs with existing equipment** — Compare complete current and proposed bills with selected existing generators or batteries. The comparison separates bill charges from operating costs and other economic assumptions.
  - **Expected generator value** — Review each generator's reliability adjustment alongside operating costs. Check any warning that marks the financial estimate as approximate before using it.
  - **Clearer study readiness** — If the missing-data check fails or is interrupted, retry it before calculating. Review the study's data warnings and operating assumptions before using its results.

  See [Scenarios & Modelling](/scenarios#energy-assessments-and-existing-equipment) and [Site energy assets](/sites#energy-assets). Generator modelling covers existing equipment; it does not assess buying a new generator or provide generator proposals or PDFs. Results depend on the available data and declared assumptions. An entered backup value does not simulate outages.

  ### Payments

  - **Select in-credit invoices** — When generating or regenerating a payment file, expand **In-credit invoices** and tick **Net on this run** to offset bills on the same Retail Account. Only eligible credits are offered, and your selection and reason are recorded.
  - **Keep credits when regenerating** — Credits already netted on an unreleased file carry forward. If one can no longer be included, UMS refuses the rebuild so you can review it rather than lose the offset.

  See [Processing Invoice Payments](/guides/processing-payments#in-credit-invoices-and-regeneration) for eligibility and regeneration details.
