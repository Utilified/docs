Stay up to date with the latest features and improvements in UMS.

<Update label="v3.32.0" description="2026-11-01">
  ## Custom reports, customer dashboards, Scenario Studio and SSO

  UMS now ships as one system version across the platform, starting at v3.0.0, so release numbers jump from v1.69.0 to v3.x. This entry covers every customer-visible change from v3.0.0 to v3.32.0.

  ### Reports and dashboards

  - **Custom reports** — Build your own reports with group-by, sorting, formulas using Site attributes, and a days measure. Large reports export in the background.
  - **Emissions in reports** — Reports split emissions by scope, add energy and Scope 3, and show the market renewable share in the basis.
  - **Customer dashboards** — Build dashboards in UMS and publish them to the Portal.
  - **Accrual report** — Accrues usage, shows Site status, applies the account filter to bills and leaves out reversed bills.
  - **Saved grid views** — Personal grid views save to your account, and you can export a grid's visible columns.

  ### Scenarios and modelling

  - **Scenario Studio** — One launcher for every measure, with run summaries and a faster portfolio sweep.
  - **Portfolio screening** — Screen batteries and power factor correction alongside solar.
  - **Seasonal charges** — Annual costs price seasonal charges over their season.

  ### Invoices and payments

  - **Supplier reading fixes** — UMS reads Origin, Shell and Alinta C&I invoices more accurately, including units, cent rates and GST.
  - **Duplicate handling** — A duplicate upload's PDF links to the stored invoice, and duplicate summaries identify the source file.
  - **Payment controls** — Payment approval is tighter, and SAP journal invoices with missing coding are held.

  ### Brokerage

  - **Unified runs** — Preview a run, check coverage and approve it before it commits and invoices.
  - **Rate types** — Three rate types plus percentage commission, with forecast run rates and due dates.
  - **Supplier statements and RCTIs** — Import supplier statements and RCTIs, including from metering providers.

  ### Metering and integrations

  - **Solar monitoring** — Connect SolisCloud and Sungrow to bring in solar data.
  - **Meters** — A new meter starts when the interval length changes, and NMI or MIRN mislabels no longer create duplicate connections.

  ### Sign-in and security

  - **Single sign-on** — Verify email domains with a DNS record and set up SSO yourself for Portal parent accounts. Sign-in starts with your email.
  - **API keys** — Organisation API keys expire and can be rotated.
  - **Sessions** — Changing your password or signing out ends your other sessions.

  ### Everyday use

  - **Global search** — Search results are grouped by type, with Ask Joule built in.
  - **Lists keep their place** — Going back to a list keeps your filters and position.
  - **Account icons** — UMS fetches an account's icon from its website, and you can upload your own.
</Update>
