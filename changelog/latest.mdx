Stay up to date with the latest features and improvements in UMS.

<Update label="v1.68.0" description="2026-10-06">
  ## Energy assessments and proposals, GST treatment checks, and a payment-prep workbench

  ### Energy assessments and proposals

  These sections appear only when energy assessments and proposals are enabled for your organisation.

  - **Energy assessments** — Run an assessment for a Site, model solar and battery options against its real usage and Tariffs, and turn the result into a proposal from the new Proposals page.
  - **Joule drafts the proposal narrative** — Joule writes the first draft of a proposal's narrative from the assessment, ready for you to edit.
  - **Assessment autopilot** — UMS studies eligible Sites automatically and queues the promising ones on a new Opportunities tab under Scenarios, with notifications linking straight to each one.
  - **Joule study copilot** — Joule shows assessment runs and solar and battery shortlists in the chat, and a readiness checklist tells you what a Site still needs before it can be assessed.
  - **Existing equipment** — A Site's new Energy assets tab records the solar, batteries and inverters already installed, and modelling now accounts for them.
  - **Certificates, replacement costs and NZ tax** — Results show the certificates a system earns, replacement costs in size bands, and New Zealand tax treatment.
  - **More demand charge types** — Modelling prices top-N, power factor, event mean and block demand charges.

  ### Invoice validation

  - **GST treatment checks** — Each line's GST treatment is checked against the jurisdiction's tax rate, and you can review and correct it in the Workbench, with UMS learning the rule for next time.
  - **Convert GST-inclusive lines** — When a supplier prints lines including GST, UMS flags it and previews each line ex-GST, and you can convert them on the Invoice's Line Items tab, in the Workbench, or in bulk from the review queue.
  - **Network tariff mismatch in review** — The review panel shows when the network Tariff on the bill differs from the one assigned, along with the billing basis and how much of the period the reads cover.
  - **Apply boundary realignments in one click** — Period realignments queued in the Workbench show each start date that moves and apply on confirm.
  - **Composite and split charges matched** — Fixed charges billed as several parts, and lines split across a statement, now match their Tariff rate.
  - **Demand days, seasons and partial reads** — Per-day demand charges count the right days, rate checks respect seasons, and periods with only partial read coverage are judged on what is there.
  - **Cleaner derived Tariffs** — Network-coded lines stay out of derived retail Tariffs, and a derived rate is never tied to another state's time-of-use periods.
  - **AER Tariff import fixes** — Energy rates without a code are no longer read as demand, and distributor demand bases and top-N daily maximums import correctly.
  - **Regulator-year boundaries** — Day-weighting across a 1 July regulator-year change is calculated correctly.

  ### Payments

  - **Payment-prep workbench** — Pre-flight exceptions for GST anomalies and Retail Account number mismatches open in a drawer with their own layouts, funnel counts, re-check, and save and next.
  - **No exception for a missing Invoice number** — An Invoice with no printed number no longer raises an exception before payment.
  - **Edit netted credit lines** — Credit lines netted on a payment file can now be edited.
  - **No zero-value lines** — Payment files never include a zero-value line.
  - **Rounding to the cent** — Amounts round half-up to the cent, and splits allocate the leftover cent so totals always add up.
  - **No payable without Connection records** — An Invoice with no Connection records no longer creates a payable.

  ### Tariffs

  - **Assign and switch Tariffs in one dialog** — Pick the Tariff and dates, and optionally the assignment it replaces, which is end-dated the day before; overlapping assignments are blocked.
  - **Edit rate bindings in the Tariff tree** — Change which rates a Tariff binds to directly from the tree.
  - **Contracted demand and MDQ** — Agreement Connections show contracted demand (kVA) and MDQ (GJ/day).

  ### Invoices

  - **Duplicate detection across formats** — A numbered spreadsheet row now matches the same bill's unnumbered PDF copy.
  - **UtiliRead accuracy** — EnergyAustralia Invoices take the printed issue date, AGL "cr" amounts read as credits, and GST-free account-level charges are included in Connection totals.
  - **Emailed interval data** — NEM12 files sent to an Account inbox go to Reads instead of being treated as Invoices.
  - **Page-limit holds stay visible** — Files held for exceeding the page limit remain visible in the inbox and import queues.

  ### Other improvements

  - **Clearer errors** — When the server refuses an action, UMS shows the reason, and confirmation dialogs look and behave the same everywhere.
  - **Data Hub time-of-use** — Time-of-use definitions show their labels with a weekday by half-hour preview, and a new Quantities list sits alongside them.
  - **Public holidays stay current** — Time-of-use windows use a backup holiday source when the government calendar is late.
  - **Smoother updates** — If UMS updates while you have it open, the page reloads instead of showing an error.
  - **Accessibility and dark mode** — Better headings, button labels and keyboard navigation, plus dark-mode colour fixes.
  - **Faster pages** — API responses are compressed, and the brokerage and Invoice lists load faster.
</Update>
