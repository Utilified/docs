Stay up to date with the latest features and improvements in UMS.

<Update label="v4.14.1" description="2026-11-05">
  ## Outage board, full grid exports, a smarter inbox and scheduled draft reports

  This release covers everything since v3.30.0.

  ### Connections

  - **Outage board** — A new Outages tab under Connections lists live, in-progress, upcoming and recently ended outages that affect your Connections. Sites and Connections show a banner while an outage is in progress or upcoming, and an Off supply tile on Home takes you straight to them.
  - **Outages refresh in place** — The board refreshes without resetting your page or scroll position, and more outage notices are matched to the right Connections.
  - **Short read histories** — Connections with less than 30 days of reads now chart and drill down.

  ### Inbox

  - **Bills attached to overdue notices** — When a supplier's reminder attaches the bill it is chasing, UMS now reads that bill.
  - **Payment receipts on the ledger** — Supplier payment receipts post as payments on the Retail Account, and the next bill's printed payments are checked against them.
  - **Fewer misfiled emails** — Overdue request letters are filed as letters, forwarded bills are read as Invoices, survey emails are archived, and emails sent as HTML are read correctly.
  - **Clearer error handling** — Excluding an email asks for a reason, reprocessing no longer risks removing Invoices, unmatched mail has its own tab, and every tab shows its count.

  ### Reports and dashboards

  - **Agreement expiry report** — Filter by period, customer, status and onboarding stage, see names instead of IDs, and optionally check that each Connection was billed across its agreement term.
  - **Schedule draft custom reports** — Schedule a custom report before you publish it, over a rolling period (current month, previous month, previous quarter or year to date) or only the rows added since the last run.
  - **Estimated emissions on dashboards** — Dashboards can show consumption-based estimated emissions when you have no saved emissions calculations, and filter by Account, state or Agreement.
  - **Emissions for portal viewers** — Portal viewers can now see emissions measures in shared custom reports.

  ### Grids

  - **Export everything you've filtered** — Download as CSV or Excel now exports every filtered row, not just the loaded page, with the columns you can see in their current order.

  ### Invoices and validation

  - **Fewer false exceptions** — Validation now handles separately priced metering, bundled network fixed charges, month-priced lines, per-day demand rates, rates billed per another period, pass-through amount-only lines and DLF-only loss factors, and ignores archived Retail Accounts.
  - **Missing charges flagged** — Validation flags other charges on a bill that aren't recorded against the Connection.
  - **Complete "All" exceptions view** — The All preset now lists every exception type.
  - **Supplier invoices** — Diamond, AGL, Alinta, Shell C&I, Stanwell and Origin SME invoices read more accurately, including GST splits and revision bills.
  - **UtiliRead duplicates** — Byte-identical copies of an Invoice are marked as duplicates instead of going to review.
  - **Invoice documents** — UMS tells you when a document can't be previewed, and non-PDF documents no longer download when the page opens.
  - **Account balances** — Account balances now include other charges and credits.

  ### Settings

  - **SSO secret expiry warnings** — Record when your portal SSO secret expires and get warnings 30 days and 7 days out and on expiry. Connection failures now show in the SSO panel.
  - **Portal analytics and chat** — Add Google Analytics 4 and Zoho SalesIQ chat to your portal from portal settings. The Google Tag Manager field has been removed.

  ### Other improvements

  - **Scenarios** — Seasonal charges are priced over their season in annual costs, and a battery's capacity starts at its shown health.
  - **Emissions** — The renewable power percentage is averaged over NGA reporting years.
  - **Dashboards** — Dashboard visuals and card sizing are restored.
  - **AusNet time of use** — AusNet network time-of-use periods follow AEST.
</Update>
