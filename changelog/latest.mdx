Stay up to date with the latest features and improvements in UMS.

<Update label="v4.42.0" description="2026-11-21">
  ## An audit log, saved views on every list, and help beside the page

  This release covers everything since v4.27.0.

  ### Settings

  - **Audit log** — Organisation admins can open Audit log in Settings, under Access & integrations, to see your organisation's sign-ins, credential changes, exports and Utilified staff access, filtered by user, event and date.
  - **Unsaved changes warning** — Settings forms, including AutoPilot settings, warn you before you leave with unsaved changes.
  - **Solar plant setup** — In Settings, Solar monitoring, Sync all sets up every unlinked SolisCloud or Sungrow plant in one grid, with fill-down for install dates. A plant with no Connection is skipped instead of blocking the batch.

  ### Lists and navigation

  - **Saved views** — Save the filters and sort you use on the Accounts, Sites, Agreements, Connections, Contacts, Suppliers, Meters and Tariffs lists.
  - **Filters in the link** — Filters, sort and page stay in the URL on more lists, including Payment groups, Data feeds and Data sources, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view.
  - **Tidier navigation** — Help and Import each have one entry in the navigation. Old links still work.
  - **Keyboard shortcuts** — Press ? anywhere to see the keyboard shortcuts.

  ### Connections, sites and accounts

  - **Billing and contract on the Connection Overview** — A Connection's Overview shows its active Retail Account and current energy and metering Agreements, so you no longer open the Accounts tab to see who bills it.
  - **Consistent detail pages** — Tabs stay in place while a page loads, count tiles open the tab that lists them, and subtitles link to the parent record. The Account overview loads faster.
  - **Generation sub-connections** — Generation sub-connections show as generation, not consumption.
  - **Modelled energy charts** — Reported zero battery discharge and zero generator output now show on charts, and modelled half-hourly supply reconciles with Site load.

  ### Invoices and inbox

  - **Copy invoice numbers** — Copy an invoice number with one click.
  - **Undo archive** — Archiving an inbox email happens straight away, with an Undo button in place of a confirm dialog.
  - **Litre units** — Invoices that spell litres as "LT" are read correctly.
  - **Clearer load failures** — Invoice, payment file and Connection pages tell you when they fail to load, and a failed Connection load can be retried.
  - **No double-submits** — Actions on detail pages, including activating and closing a Retail Account, can't submit twice or report success when they failed.

  ### ESG

  - **ESG reporting** — Prepare your data for NGER, NPI, ASRS S2 and GHG Protocol in one step, check each framework's readiness checklist, and export multi-sheet XLSX files. NGER thresholds now match the Clean Energy Regulator's. The ESG section appears only when it is enabled for your organisation.

  ### Help

  - **Contact Utilified beside the page** — Contact Utilified opens beside the page you need help with and already knows any error that page showed.
  - **Documentation links** — The ten busiest screens link to their documentation.

  ### Other improvements

  - **Printing** — Printing or saving as PDF gives you the page without the sidebar and toolbars.
  - **Connection lost banner** — A banner tells you when your internet connection drops.
  - **Exact timestamps** — Hover over a relative time such as "2 hours ago" to see the exact time.
  - **Shorter wizards** — Wizards skip steps the page you started from already answers.
  - **Accessibility** — Higher contrast for secondary buttons, count badges, approvals and Joule links, and Joule respects reduced motion.
  - **Supplier types** — The Transmission Operator supplier type is labelled correctly.
</Update>
