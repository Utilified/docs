The Settings area provides administrative controls for your UMS organisation.

## Navigating Settings

Settings uses a left-hand navigation rail split into two groups:

- **Personal** — settings scoped to your own account, such as your profile and preferences.
- **Organisation** — settings that affect everyone in your organisation, such as users, roles, and bulk imports.

The rail is permission-aware: sections you don't have permission to view are **hidden**, not greyed out. If you're expecting to see a section and it's missing from the rail, contact your organisation administrator to check your role.

## User management

Manage user accounts, roles, and permissions from the **Users** section:

- **Add users** — Invite new team members to your organisation
- **Edit roles** — Assign permissions that control what each user can access
- **Deactivate users** — Remove access for users who no longer need it

For a step-by-step walkthrough, see [Setting Up a User](/guides/setting-up-a-user).

## Imports and exports

Bulk data management tools:

- **Bulk imports** — Upload accounts, sites, connections, and other data in bulk using CSV templates
- **Import history** — View the status and results of previous imports
- **Export data** — Download data from any module as CSV

To import data, see [Bulk Importing Data](/guides/bulk-importing-data).

## Preferences

Customise your UMS experience:

- **Display settings** — Configure default views and layouts
- **Organisation settings** — Manage organisation-wide configuration
