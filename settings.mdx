The Settings area gathers your personal preferences and your organisation's configuration in one place. Open it from your avatar in the top-right corner — choose **My account** for personal settings or **Organisation** for organisation settings — then use the left nav rail at `/settings`.

## Navigating Settings

Settings uses a left-hand navigation rail split into two groups:

- **Personal** — settings scoped to your own account, such as your profile, security, and preferences.
- **Organisation** — settings that affect everyone in your organisation, such as users, automation, and billing.

<Note>
  The rail is permission-aware: sections you don't have permission to view are **hidden**, not greyed out. Organisation sections require manager or admin access, and some (Portal & Domain, Single Sign-On) also require a Business or Enterprise plan. If you're expecting a section and it's missing, contact your organisation administrator.
</Note>

## Personal

Settings scoped to you. See [Personal Settings](/settings/personal) for the full detail.

| Section | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Your name, contact details, and profile picture |
| **Security** | Password and two-factor authentication (2FA) |
| **Appearance** | Light, dark, or system theme |
| **Notifications** | Your notification preferences and subscriptions |
| **Alert Routing** | Rules that forward alerts to specific contacts |
