Dashboards let you build views of spend and usage and publish them to the white-label portal for the accounts they are for. Custom measures add your own formulas for any dashboard to use.

Go to **Reports** and select the **Dashboards** tab (`/dashboards`).

<Note>
  The **Dashboards** and **Custom measures** tabs only appear when your role includes the dashboards permission. Creating, duplicating, and deleting dashboards each need the matching permission.
</Note>

## The dashboards list

The grid shows each dashboard's **Name**, **Status**, **Audience** (how many accounts it is published to), **Template**, and **Updated** date. Row actions let you **Duplicate** a dashboard (the copy starts as a draft) or **Delete** it. Deleting a dashboard removes it for portal users too.

**Status** is one of:

- **Draft** — not yet published
- **Published** — live in the portal
- **Draft differs from published** — published, with unpublished edits

## Creating a dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new dashboard">
    Click **New dashboard** and enter a **Name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a starting layout">
    Leave **Start with a standard layout** ticked to begin with a set of spend blocks, or untick it to start empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add blocks">
    Use the block buttons above the canvas: **KPI**, **Line chart**, **Bar chart**, **Table**, **Ranking**, and **Text**. Drag a block to move it, or use the arrow keys (Shift+arrows to resize). Click **Edit block** to configure it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the audience">
    Click **Settings** and choose the **Audience**. Portal users of these accounts, and of every account beneath them, see the dashboard once published.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish**. After later edits, click **Publish changes** to update what customers see.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Changes save automatically while you edit.

## Dashboard settings

Click **Settings** to open the settings drawer:

- **Name** and **Description**
- **Audience** — the accounts the dashboard is published to
- **Template** — marks the dashboard as a starting point to duplicate for other accounts
- **Dashboard filters** — filters applied to every block
- **Filters viewers can change** — choose which of **Period**, **Site**, **Utility**, and **Site tag** portal users can adjust

## Previewing and unpublishing

Click **View as customer** to see the published dashboard as a portal user does. Click **Unpublish** to take it out of the portal; portal users stop seeing it straight away.

## Custom measures

The **Custom measures** tab (`/dashboards/measures`) holds formulas over spend, consumption, demand, emissions, and [site data](/site-data) that any dashboard can use.

Click **New measure** and fill in:

- **Name** and **Key**
- **Formula**
- **Format** — **Number**, **Currency**, or **Percent (0.25 shows as 25%)**
- **Decimals** and **Unit label** (for example, `$/kWh`)

The grid lists each measure's **Name**, **Key**, **Formula**, **Format**, and **Unit**.
