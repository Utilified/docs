UMS notifies you about exports, imports, validations and the alerts you follow. Notifications appear in a drawer you can open from anywhere, and on the **Notifications** page.

## The notifications drawer

The bell beside **Search…** at the top of the sidebar shows your unread count. Click it to open the drawer. You can also open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Notifications**. A red dot on your avatar means you have unread notifications.

In the drawer:

- Switch between **All** and **Unread**.
- Click **Refresh** to fetch new notifications.
- Click **Mark All Read** to clear the unread count.
- Click a notification to mark it as read and open what it's about.

## The Notifications page

The **Notifications** page at `/notifications` shows your totals and the full list. Open it from **View all** on the **Notifications** module on [Home](/home).

| Panel | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | **Total** and **Unread** counts, and whether **Desktop notifications** are **On** or **Off** in this browser. Click **Mark all as read** to clear the unread count. |
| **By priority** | How many notifications you have at each priority: **Urgent**, **High**, **Normal** and **Low**. |
| **All notifications** | Every notification, with unread ones in bold. Click **Load more** to see older ones. |

Clicking a notification marks it as read and takes you to what it's about. **Notification settings** at the top of the page opens your preferences.

## Choosing what you receive

Your preferences are in **Settings** › **Notifications**, in the **Account** group. See [My account](/settings/personal#notifications).

- **Desktop notifications** shows notifications on your desktop even when no Utilified tab is open. Turn it on separately in each browser.
- **Notification types** lists the notifications you can set, with an **Email** and an **In-app** box for each. A dash means that notification isn't sent on that channel. Click **Save changes** to keep your choices.

Some notifications only go to people who have switched them on. For example, agreement expiry warnings go out 90, 60 and 30 days before an agreement ends, and only to people who have switched them on and are assigned to that agreement's account.

To send alerts to your contacts by event type, use [Alert routing](/settings/organisation#alert-routing).
