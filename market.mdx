Select **Market** in the **Commercial** group of the sidebar (`/market`).

Market brings wholesale energy prices, forward prices, computed market signals, supply changes and news into UMS. Use it to follow the market and explain movements to your customers.

## Tabs

| Tab          | Route              | What it holds                                                                     |
| ------------ | ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Desk**     | `/market`          | What is happening now: status, signals, the price board, a chart, regions, news and supply |
| **News**     | `/market/news`     | Market news and market notices, with filters                                      |
| **Forwards** | `/market/forwards` | Futures contracts and strips, settlement history and forward curves               |
| **Insights** | `/market/insights` | Signals, region statistics and price shape                                        |
| **Supply**   | `/market/supply`   | Generation capacity leaving and arriving, and the supply register                 |
| **Analyse**  | `/market/analyse`  | A custom chart of up to six series, with notes and export                         |

## Choosing a market

Every tab starts with the market bar. Pick a market by its code, or **All markets**. The bar also shows the market's time, its trading sessions (**Open**, **Closed** or **Continuous**), the **Last settlement** date, and **Feeds**: how many price series are on time and how many are late against their usual publishing pattern. On the **Desk**, click the late count to highlight the late prices on the board.

## Desk

The **Desk** reads top to bottom in order of urgency:

- Active signals: a strip of **Alert** and **Watch** signals for the market, with a link to the rest in **Insights**. It only shows when a signal is active.
- **Prices**: the quote board. Rows are grouped into **Spot**, **Gas**, **Fuels and FX**, and **Futures** (WA shows Synergy's offered curve instead). With **All markets** selected, each group is named for its market. The **1D**, **1W**, **1M**, **3M** and **1Y** toggle sets the change period for every row. A price that is behind its usual schedule shows **Late**; hover it to see when the last price landed. Click a row to chart it; a futures row opens **Forwards** instead.
- **Today in the market**: a daily summary of the market, with numbered links to its sources. Expand it to read the whole brief.
- The chart: with nothing selected, all regions' prices for the market (**All markets** or the market's prices). Switch between **Full scale** and **Zoom** (cuts off spikes), pick **7D**, **30D** or **90D** or **From** / **To** dates, and use the export menu to **Download CSV** or **Download PNG**. Selecting an instrument charts it alone, with a back button to return.
- **Regions**: each region's latest spot price, with **High**, **Low**, **Spread** and **Simple average**. The expand button (**Open the live grid**) shows a larger map.
- **Recent news**: the latest headlines, filtered to the selected instrument where possible. **All news** opens the **News** tab.
- **Next in supply**: the next capacity **Leaving** and **Arriving, firm**. **Supply** opens the **Supply** tab.

On narrower screens the rail panels stack under the chart and news moves into a drawer behind the news button.

## News

The **News** tab opens with **Today in the market**, then filters:

- Category buttons: **All news** plus categories such as **Regulatory**, **Policy**, **Projects**, **Prices**, **Corporate**, **Analysis** and **General**, each with its count
- **Search news**
- **Region**, **Commodity** (**Coal**, **Gas**, **Renewables**) and **Source**
- **Dates**, a date range
- **Clear filters**, once any filter is set

Below the filters are **Top stories**, the **Latest** stream and a **Market notices** panel. Click **Load more** for older items.

## Forwards

The **Forwards** tab gathers forward prices:

- **Contracts**: quarterly base-load contracts by **Region**, and the **Strips** (financial-year and calendar-year) where traded. Click a contract to chart its history.
- **Settlement history**: every region's futures settlements over time, or one contract's when selected
- **Forward curves**: each region's quarterly base-load curve against the curve **1 week ago**, **1 month ago** and **3 months ago**

WA prices are Synergy's offered curve and are labelled that way. A market with no forward prices says so.

## Insights

The **Insights** tab holds computed facts about the selected market:

- **Signals**: rule checks on prices, feeds and announced closures, each **Alert**, **Watch** or **Info**
- **Region statistics**: spot price statistics per region over **7D**, **30D**, **90D** or **1Y**: **Avg**, **Peak**, **Off-peak**, **P5**, **P95**, **Max**, **Neg.**, **High**, volatility and, where relevant, **Coverage** and **Z vs 1y**. Hover a column heading for its definition. Select a region to see its price shape.
- **Price shape**: for one region over **30D**, **90D**, **1Y** or **2Y**, an **Average price by hour and month** heat map and a **Duration curve**

## Supply

The **Supply** tab compares generation capacity leaving the market with capacity arriving.

- **Leaving and arriving**: headline **Leaving**, **Firm arriving** and **Net** capacity, with the **Next exit** and **Next firm arrival**. Firm means committed, under construction or commissioning. Turn on **Pipeline** to include projects that aren't firm yet. The **Region** filter scopes the whole tab.
- **Register**: closures and new projects of 100 MW or more, each linked to its primary source. Narrow it by side (**All**, **Arriving**, **Leaving**), **Status**, fuel or technology, **From** / **To** dates, and **Search**.

## Analyse

The **Analyse** tab builds a custom chart.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick series">
    Choose up to six instruments in **Series**: spot regions, quarterly futures and other indices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shape the chart">
    Choose **Absolute** or **Indexed** (rebased to 100 at the start), an aggregation period (**Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly** or **Quarterly**), a statistic (**Avg**, **Max** or **Min**), and the date range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add notes">
    Click **Add note**, click a date on the chart, type the **Note**, and click **Add to chart**. Notes appear as markers on the chart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Use the export menu to **Download PNG** of the chart or **Download CSV** of its data. Notes are included in the CSV.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your organisation has portal branding, switch between **App theme** and your organisation's branding so an exported chart matches your portal.

For a futures series, the tab also lists known forward-looking factors for that delivery period, such as scheduled closures and policy milestones.
