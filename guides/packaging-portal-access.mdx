Client plans let you sell your customer portal in tiers. A plan decides which portal features a client gets, which ones they can see but have to ask for, and how you charge for it. This guide walks through setting plans up and running them.

You need to be an org admin, on a plan that includes the portal. Charging through brokerage and billing service fees also need the Brokerage permission.

## 1. Define your plans

Go to **Settings** › **Portal & domain** › **Client plans** and click **New plan**. See [Client plans](/settings/portal-domain#client-plans) for every field.

For each plan, set every portal feature to one of three states:

- **Included**: the client has the feature.
- **Locked**: the client sees the feature in their menu with a lock, and can ask you for a quote.
- **Hidden**: the client doesn't see the feature at all.

Lock the features you want to upsell. A client on a lower plan then sees what the next plan adds, and can ask for it from inside the portal. Hide the features that don't suit the plan's audience.

<Note>
  A locked feature shows only its name and description. It never shows the client's data, and the quote page doesn't show plan names or prices.
</Note>

## 2. Choose how to charge

Each plan has a **Price**, a **Billing period** of **Monthly** or **Annual**, and a **Charge mode**:

| Charge mode | Use it when | What you set up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Brokerage** | You already earn brokerage on the client's energy or metering contract, and part of that rate pays for the portal | A brokerage recovery on each client's account (step 4) |
| **Service fee** | The client has no brokered agreement, or you bill the portal separately | Nothing per client. UMS generates a fee every month (step 6) |
| **None** | The portal is free, or you bill it outside UMS | Nothing |

## 3. Assign plans to clients

Open the client's account, go to its **Settings** tab and find **Portal Access**. Choose the plan in **Plan** and click **Save Portal Access**. See [Portal access](/accounts#portal-access).

To give every client without settings of its own the same plan, choose it under **Default portal access** in **Settings** › **Portal & domain**, then click **Save Portal Defaults**.

**Custom** in the **Plan** picker keeps the per-feature switches, for a client that doesn't fit any plan.

<Tip>
  If your client records live in a CRM, connect it and let a field on each CRM account choose the plan. See [CRM](/settings/organisation#crm). Plans the CRM sets show **Managed by** on the account.
</Tip>

## 4. Set up brokerage recovery

For a client on a **Brokerage** plan, open **Portal Access** on their account after saving the plan. In the **Brokerage recovery** panel, click **Add recovery**. Choose the agreement whose brokerage contract pays for the plan, and enter the **Component Rate**: the part of the contract rate that covers the portal, in the contract's units.

For example, say a contract earns 0.5 c/kWh and you agreed that 0.1 c/kWh of it pays for the portal. Enter 0.1 c/kWh in the contract's rate unit. UMS then reports a fifth of that contract's revenue as portal plan revenue. The contract and its invoices to the retailer don't change.

See [Brokerage recovery](/accounts#brokerage-recovery) for the warnings a recovery can show.

## 5. Handle quote requests

When a client clicks **Request a quote** on a locked feature, the people linked to their account get an in-app notification and an email. If nobody is linked, your org admins get it. The request appears under **Requests** in the account's **Portal Access** section.

1. Talk to the client, then click **Mark contacted**.
2. If they say yes, click **Mark won**. Choose a plan that includes the feature under **New plan** and click **Switch plan**. The client gets the feature straight away.
3. If they decline, click **Mark lost**.

See [Quote requests](/accounts#quote-requests).

## 6. Bill service fees

UMS generates last month's service fees each day. To bill them:

1. Go to **Brokerage** › **Invoices & RCTIs** › **Service fees**.
2. Pick the **Month** and check the fees. Click **Generate** if you need to create a month's fees yourself.
3. Select the fees and click **Raise in Xero**. UMS creates one draft invoice in Xero per client account. Approve and send them in Xero.

Set the Xero account code and tax type for these lines under **Portal service fees** in your Xero invoice field mapping. See [Service fees](/brokerage#service-fees) for voiding fees, exporting and what to do if Xero times out.

## 7. See the revenue

On the **Brokerage** **Overview**, the **Portal plans via brokerage** tile shows this month's plan revenue from brokerage and the year to date. On **Analytics**, **Revenue by source** charts the last 12 months, split into brokerage, portal plans via brokerage and service fees. For other periods, run the **Revenue by source** report. See [Revenue by source](/brokerage#revenue-by-source).

## Things to know

- A plan in use can't be deleted. Move its clients to another plan first.
- An inactive plan stays on the clients already using it, with all its features. You can't assign it to anyone new.
- Service fees use the plan's current price, so a price change applies from the next fee generated.
- Every account on a service-fee plan is billed, whether or not anyone uses the portal.
