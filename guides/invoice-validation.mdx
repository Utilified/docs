This guide walks through validating an invoice, clearing the **Needs attention** queue, and disputing a billing error until it is credited. For what each screen shows, see [Validation](/validation).

## Validate an invoice

To validate a single invoice:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invoice">
    Go to **Invoices** › **Invoices** and click the invoice number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run validation">
    Click **Validate this invoice** in the header, then **Run validation**. **Re-run with Joule detectors** runs it again with Joule's extra checks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the result">
    When the run finishes, the **Review** tab shows each line beside its finding. The tab badge counts unresolved errors.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Validate this invoice** only appears if you can update validation.
</Note>

To re-run a whole set, open **Invoices** › **Validations**, narrow the queue to the invoices you want, and click **Run validation for these invoices**.

## Clear the queue

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the queue">
    Go to **Invoices** › **Validations**. The queue opens on **Errors found**, the runs still carrying an unanswered error. **Money at stake** shows how much those errors are worth.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow it down">
    Pick a saved view such as **Errors over $500**, or filter by **Supplier**, **Status** or **Error variance at least**. Sort by **Error variance** to work the largest errors first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a run">
    Click a row. The review panel shows the error variance, the tariff basis and every finding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide the outcome">
    - **Accept variance** if the charge is right. Tick **Record the variance as justifiable so validation learns from it** when the variance is legitimate.
    - **Fix tariff** if the expected price was wrong, then **Re-run validation**.
    - **Hold payment** if the bill must not be paid yet.
    - **Record billing error** if the retailer overcharged or undercharged. See [Dispute a billing error](#dispute-a-billing-error).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move to the next run">
    Press <kbd>j</kbd> for the next run and <kbd>k</kbd> for the previous. <kbd>a</kbd>, <kbd>f</kbd>, <kbd>h</kbd> and <kbd>r</kbd> accept, fix the tariff, hold and re-run.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Clear **Awaiting tariff confirmation** the same way: check the tariff with **Fix tariff**, then re-run.

### Bulk actions

Select several rows and choose **Accept selected**, **Re-run selected** or **Hold selected**. Each asks you to confirm, and a banner reports what succeeded.

## Dispute a billing error

A billing error records a charge the retailer got wrong and follows it until it is credited.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Record the error">
    From a failed or needs-review run, click **Record billing error**. You can also do this on the **Dispute** tab of the validation run, on the invoice's **Review** tab, or from a **Variance** exception on the **Exceptions** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Tick **Overcharge**, **Undercharge** or both. A direction the run's findings support is ticked and pre-filled for you, and a direction that already has an open error isn't offered. For each ticked direction, check the **Amount (incl. GST)** and **Reason**, and turn on **Hold this bill from payment until it is resolved** to stop it being paid. Add a **Note** if you need to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Click **Record**, or **Record and mark raised** if you have already told the retailer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write to the retailer (optional)">
    On the run's **Dispute** tab, click **Generate Dispute Letter**. Edit the draft, then **Copy** or **Download** it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track the outcome">
    As the retailer responds, use the error's card to **Mark raised**, then **Retailer accepted**, then **Record credit**. Use **Retailer rejected** or **Abandon** if it goes no further.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To see every open error, click **Billing errors** on the **Validations** tab. The tracker groups them into **Needs action**, **With retailer**, **Awaiting credit** and **Closed**. See [Billing errors](/invoice-exceptions#billing-errors).
