---
title: "Tracking Metering Coverage"
description: "Use the Metering Coverage report in UMS to find large-market NMIs with no metering agreement, metering agreements that have started but aren't earning, and revenue that has stopped."
---

The **Metering Coverage** report lists every large-market NEM electricity connection on an active retail contract you brokered. For each one it shows whether there's a metering agreement and whether that agreement is earning. Use it to find metering opportunities and to chase metering revenue that hasn't arrived.

<Note>
  The report only appears for users with access to the **Brokerage** module, because it shows received brokerage revenue.
</Note>

## What the report shows

Each row is one connection (NMI). The report includes connections that are:

- Top-level, active, NEM electricity connections
- Classified as large customers, or not yet classified
- On an active electricity or energy retail agreement that has a brokerage record. Contracts with no brokerage record, such as a customer's direct contract, are left out unless you turn off **Brokered retail contracts only**.

The **coverage status** column says what to do next:

| Coverage status      | Meaning                                                                 | Typical action                                          |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| `no_metering`        | No current metering agreement                                           | Offer a metering agreement                              |
| `signed_not_started` | A metering agreement is signed but hasn't started yet                   | No action; check again after the start date             |
| `started_not_live`   | The metering agreement has started but no revenue has been received     | Ask the metering provider to confirm the install        |
| `stopped`            | Revenue was received, but nothing has been paid for more than 120 days | Chase the provider's supplier statements (RCTIs)        |
| `live`               | Revenue is being received                                               | None                                                    |
| `unclassified`       | The customer size is unknown and there's no metering agreement         | Set the connection's NMI classification                 |

Other columns include the account, site, NMI, state, classification, annual consumption, retailer, retail agreement, and the retail account number with its status (for example `ACTIVE` or `INACTIVE`). They also include the metering provider and agreement, onboarding stage, metering start and in-service dates, revenue received and the last paid period.

<Tip>
  Metering revenue normally starts only once the meter is installed, and supplier statements can lag by around two months. A `started_not_live` row with a recent start date usually just needs time.
</Tip>

## Running the report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Reports">
    Go to **Reports** and find **Metering Coverage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Run and choose filters">
    Filter by **Coverage status** (one or more), **Customer** (sub-accounts are included), **Metering provider**, or **Active retail account only**. Leave the filters empty to see every row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the result">
    Choose Excel, CSV or JSON. You'll get a notification when it's ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Scheduling it

Click **Schedule** on the report to receive it weekly. Create one schedule per audience, for example:

- **Sales:** coverage status `no_metering`
- **Operations:** coverage statuses `started_not_live` and `stopped`, filtered by metering provider if you forward each provider its own list
- **Management:** all statuses

## Keeping the report accurate

The report is only as good as the data behind it. When you set up a new contract:

- **Create the brokerage record** on the retail agreement. Without one, the connection is left out of the report.
- **Set the agreement's segment** (large market, small market or embedded network).
- **Set the NMI classification** (large or small customer) on each connection. Without it, UMS estimates size from annual consumption against the state's large-customer threshold. With no consumption data either, a connection on a small-market contract is treated as a small customer, unless it has a metering agreement or a large-market contract. Anything else appears as `unclassified`.

Install dates fill in automatically as metering revenue is billed and supplier statements are accepted, so you don't need to enter them.
